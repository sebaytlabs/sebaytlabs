# Sebayt Labs

*Sebayt* is the ancient Egyptian word for teachings. We build small, plain tools that do one job well, and we give them away. They're free, open source (MIT), work offline and need no account.

**Why we build.** So many everyday tools are either rented by the month, harder to use than the job they do, or quietly built to keep you inside them. We think the default should be better: tools that are free, plain, private, and good at one job. Every repo here started as a real problem in our own work. Each README tells that story: *why it exists, what it replaces, why it works the way it does, and where it falls short*.

Each one is a desktop app with its own window, and the first time you open it a short tutorial walks you through every part, one real click at a time. Everything here is sorted the PARA way: **Projects** we're actively shipping, **Areas** we keep looking after, **Resources** to borrow from, and **Archives** for what's finished.

## How they fit together

```
            ┌──────────────── SimpleBrain ────────────────┐
            │   notes · tasks · calendar · Today · inbox  │
            └──────┬───────────────┬───────────────┬──────┘
                   │               │               │
          Learning Kit        Day Blocks       Drive Kit
     read & remember books   your ideal day   your files, backed up
                   │                               │
         Transcribe Folder                   Clip Pipeline
       lectures & talks → text         recordings → clips & captions
```

- **Learning:** transcribe a course, then turn it into questions, cards and quizzes, and keep the notes in SimpleBrain.
- **Time:** your fixed rhythm in Day Blocks, and what changes from day to day in SimpleBrain's Today.
- **Files:** Drive Kit backs up and tidies what everything else creates.
- **Sharing:** Clip Pipeline turns your recordings into posts, and the theme runs the shop.
